The tappable next-action Nexus proposes under a message. Stack `block` variants full-width under mobile assistant bubbles; use `outline` pills inline on desktop. Icons are line-icons, never emoji.

```jsx
<SuggestionButton icon={<EditIcon />}>Catalog more Hallway items</SuggestionButton>
<SuggestionButton icon={<SendIcon />}>Catalog Storage</SuggestionButton>
```
