# Nexus Moves — Beta Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test Nexus Moves! You'll catalog your own home with the app, then tell us what it got right and wrong. Your scans become our "golden set" — the benchmark we measure every future improvement against. Expect to spend about 30–45 minutes.

---

## 1. Install the app

1. You'll get an email invite from **TestFlight** (Apple's beta app store). Open it on your iPhone.
2. Install the **TestFlight** app from the App Store if you don't have it.
3. In TestFlight, tap **Accept**, then **Install** next to **Nexus Moves**.
4. Open Nexus Moves from your home screen.

## 2. Sign up — use the special email tag

When the app asks for your email, sign up with your normal email address **plus `+goldenset` before the @**:

> If your email is `john@gmail.com`, sign up as **`john+goldenset@gmail.com`**

The login code still arrives in your normal inbox (Gmail and most providers treat it as the same address) — the tag just marks your account as a golden-set tester on our side.

Enter the 6-digit code from the email and you're in.

## 3. Meet Nexus

The whole app is a chat with **Nexus**, an AI moving assistant. It will introduce itself and ask a few setup questions — your name, the address you're "moving from" (use your real one), whether it's a house or apartment, bedrooms and bathrooms. Just answer in plain language, one message at a time. You're pretending to plan a move; the goal is to build a complete inventory of what you own.

## 4. Scan your rooms — this is the important part

For each room, tap the **+** button and record a **video walkthrough** (or pick one from your library). How you shoot it matters a lot:

- **Talk while you film.** Narrate out loud what you're seeing: *"That's the queen bed, two nightstands, the tall dresser — the books on that shelf are coming, the shelf stays."* Nexus listens to the audio and uses it for names and details.
- **Lights on, phone sideways** (landscape).
- **Pan slowly.** A whole room should take 30–90 seconds. If you rush, things get missed.
- **Open closets, cabinets, and drawers** and film inside them.
- One room per video. Say which room it is in the note before you send ("This is the master bedroom").

For **big items** (sofa, fridge, piano, dining table), also take a **straight-on photo** of each — you can attach up to 5 photos at once and send them together.

After you send, a **review card** pops up with everything Nexus detected. Look at it carefully — then add the items to your inventory.

## 5. Grade the scan — this is what makes the golden set

Right after each scan, **before** you fix anything:

1. **Screenshot the review card** (all of it — scroll if needed).
2. In a notes app (or the shared sheet, if you got a link), jot down for that room:
   - **Missed:** things in the room the scan didn't find
   - **Made up:** things it listed that aren't actually there
   - **Duplicates:** the same item listed twice
   - **Wrong name/count:** e.g. it said "3 chairs" and there are 6, or called your armoire a "cabinet"
   - Things that **shouldn't count** for a move (it should already skip built-ins like toilets and ceiling lights — tell us if any sneak through)
3. Then fix the list on the review card (uncheck wrong items) and add the rest.

An honest "it missed the rug and invented a second TV" is worth more to us than a polite thumbs-up.

## 6. Try to break things

Once your rooms are in, poke around:

- Ask Nexus questions: "What's in my kitchen?", "How big a truck do I need?", "Find duplicate items."
- Tap the progress bar at the top and follow the **Next Steps** checklist.
- Try the **Share** button at the end — it makes a link a moving company could see.
- Send a photo of something weird (a wine bottle, a pet, a mirror) and see what happens.
- Background the app mid-scan, come back, force-quit and reopen. Nothing should be lost.

## 7. When something goes wrong

If the app does anything confusing, wrong, or broken: **screenshot it** and note what you did right before. Send screenshots + notes to Owen. Error messages, weird replies from Nexus, items that vanished, being logged out — all of it is gold.
